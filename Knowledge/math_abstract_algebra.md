# Abstract Algebra Reference

## Groups
- **Definition**: Set G with operation * satisfying:
  1. Closure: a*b ∈ G.
  2. Associativity: (a*b)*c = a*(b*c).
  3. Identity: e*a = a*e = a.
  4. Inverse: a*a⁻¹ = e.
- **Abelian Group**: Commutative (a*b = b*a).
- **Order**: Number of elements in G.
- **Subgroup**: Subset H ⊆ G that is itself a group.
- **Lagrange’s Theorem**: Order of subgroup divides order of group.
- **Cyclic Group**: Generated by single element. Z_n under addition mod n.
- **Permutation Group**: Symmetric group S_n. All bijections of {1,...,n}.

## Rings
- **Definition**: Set R with two operations (+, *) satisfying:
  1. (R, +) is abelian group.
  2. (R, *) is associative.
  3. Distributive laws: a*(b+c) = a*b + a*c.
- **Commutative Ring**: Multiplication is commutative.
- **Ring with Unity**: Has multiplicative identity 1.
- **Integral Domain**: Commutative ring with unity, no zero divisors.
- **Field**: Integral domain where every non-zero element has multiplicative inverse. Examples: Q, R, C, Z_p (p prime).

## Fields & Extensions
- **Finite Field (Galois Field)**: GF(p^n). p prime. Used in cryptography (AES, ECC).
- **Polynomial Ring**: F[x]. Polynomials with coefficients in field F.
- **Irreducible Polynomial**: Cannot be factored into lower-degree polynomials.
- **Field Extension**: E/F. E contains F. Degree [E:F] = dimension of E as vector space over F.

## Vector Spaces
- **Definition**: Set V over field F with addition and scalar multiplication.
- **Basis**: Linearly independent spanning set.
- **Dimension**: Number of vectors in basis.
- **Linear Transformation**: T: V → W. Preserves addition/scalar multiplication. Matrix representation.

## Homomorphisms & Isomorphisms
- **Homomorphism**: Map φ: G → H preserving structure (φ(a*b) = φ(a)*φ(b)).
- **Kernel**: Elements mapping to identity. Normal subgroup.
- **Isomorphism**: Bijective homomorphism. Structures are essentially same.
- **First Isomorphism Theorem**: G/Ker(φ) ≅ Im(φ).

## Galois Theory
- **Galois Group**: Group of automorphisms of field extension fixing base field.
- **Solvability by Radicals**: Polynomial solvable iff Galois group is solvable.
- **Abel-Ruffini Theorem**: General quintic equation not solvable by radicals.
