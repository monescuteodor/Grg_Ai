# Modern Physics & Statistical Mechanics Reference

## Quantum Physics Phenomena
### Photoelectric Effect
- **Observation**: Light shining on metal ejects electrons.
- **Key Facts**:
  - Kinetic energy depends on frequency f, not intensity.
  - Threshold frequency f₀ below which no emission occurs.
  - Instantaneous emission.
- **Explanation**: Light consists of photons with energy E=hf.
- **Equation**: K_max = hf - Φ. Φ is work function (minimum energy to escape).

### Compton Scattering
- **Observation**: X-rays scatter off electrons, wavelength increases.
- **Explanation**: Photon-electron collision conserves energy and momentum.
- **Shift**: Δλ = (h/m_e c)(1 - cosθ). Confirms particle nature of light.

### De Broglie Waves
- **Hypothesis**: Matter has wave properties. λ = h/p = h/mv.
- **Evidence**: Electron diffraction (Davisson-Germer experiment).
- **Uncertainty Principle**: ΔxΔp ≥ ℏ/2. Cannot know position and momentum simultaneously with arbitrary precision.

## Statistical Mechanics
### Microstates & Macrostates
- **Microstate**: Specific configuration of all particles (positions, momenta).
- **Macrostate**: Macroscopic properties (P, V, T). Many microstates correspond to one macrostate.
- **Multiplicity Ω**: Number of microstates for a given macrostate.
- **Entropy**: S = k_B ln Ω. Measure of disorder/probability.

### Boltzmann Distribution
- **Probability**: P(E) ∝ e^(-E/k_B T). Higher energy states less probable at low T.
- **Partition Function**: Z = Σ e^(-E_i/k_B T). Normalization constant.
- **Average Energy**: <E> = -∂(ln Z)/∂β. β = 1/k_B T.

### Ideal Gas from Stat Mech
- **Pressure**: P = (1/3)(N/V)m<v²>.
- **Temperature**: Average kinetic energy per molecule = (3/2)k_B T.
- **Maxwell-Boltzmann Distribution**: Distribution of speeds in gas. v_rms = √(3k_B T/m).

## Condensed Matter Physics
### Solids & Bands
- **Crystal Lattice**: Regular arrangement of atoms. Unit cell repeats.
- **Band Theory**:
  - Valence Band: Highest occupied energy band.
  - Conduction Band: Lowest unoccupied band.
  - Band Gap: Energy difference between bands.
- **Conductors**: Overlapping bands or partially filled band.
- **Insulators**: Large band gap (>3 eV).
- **Semiconductors**: Small band gap (~1 eV). Conductivity increases with T. Doping (n-type, p-type).

### Superconductivity
- **Zero Resistance**: Below critical temperature T_c.
- **Meissner Effect**: Expels magnetic fields. Perfect diamagnetism.
- **BCS Theory**: Electrons form Cooper pairs via lattice vibrations (phonons). Pairs behave as bosons, condense into ground state.

## Nuclear Physics
### Radioactivity
- **Alpha Decay**: Emits He nucleus (²⁴He). Z decreases by 2, A by 4.
- **Beta Decay**:
  - β⁻: Neutron → Proton + Electron + Antineutrino. Z increases by 1.
  - β⁺: Proton → Neutron + Positron + Neutrino. Z decreases by 1.
- **Gamma Decay**: Emits high-energy photon. No change in Z or A. Excited nucleus relaxes.
- **Half-Life**: t₁/₂ = ln(2)/λ. Time for half nuclei to decay. N(t) = N₀e^(-λt).

### Nuclear Reactions
- **Fission**: Heavy nucleus splits into lighter ones + neutrons + energy. Chain reaction possible. Used in nuclear power.
- **Fusion**: Light nuclei combine to form heavier one + energy. Powers stars. Requires high T/P to overcome Coulomb barrier.
- **Binding Energy**: Energy required to disassemble nucleus. Peak at Iron (Fe-56). Fusion releases energy up to Fe; Fission releases energy down to Fe.
