# Nuclear & Particle Physics Reference

## Nuclear Structure
### Liquid Drop Model
- **Binding Energy Formula**: B(A,Z) = a_v A - a_s A^{2/3} - a_c Z(Z-1)/A^{1/3} - a_a (A-2Z)²/A + δ(A,Z)
  - Volume term: Proportional to A. Strong force saturation.
  - Surface term: Correction for nucleons on surface.
  - Coulomb term: Proton-proton repulsion.
  - Asymmetry term: Pauli exclusion favors N≈Z.
  - Pairing term: δ > 0 for even-even, δ < 0 for odd-odd nuclei.
- **Applications**: Predicts stability, fission barriers, binding energy trends.

### Shell Model
- **Concept**: Nucleons move in independent orbits in mean potential well.
- **Magic Numbers**: 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126. Closed shells → extra stability.
- **Spin-Orbit Coupling**: Strong splitting of levels. Explains magic numbers.
- **Isospin**: Protons and neutrons treated as two states of same particle (nucleon). Isospin T=1/2.

## Radioactive Decay
### Alpha Decay
- **Process**: Emission of ⁴He nucleus. Q-value > 0 required.
- **Geiger-Nuttall Law**: log(λ) ∝ -1/√Q. Higher Q → shorter half-life.
- **Tunneling**: Alpha particle tunnels through Coulomb barrier. Gamow factor determines probability.

### Beta Decay
- **Weak Interaction**: Mediated by W± bosons.
- **β⁻ Decay**: n → p + e⁻ + ν̄_e. Q = [M(A,Z) - M(A,Z+1)]c².
- **β⁺ Decay**: p → n + e⁺ + ν_e. Requires Q > 2m_e c² (mass difference must create positron).
- **Electron Capture**: p + e⁻ → n + ν_e. Alternative to β⁺ when Q < 2m_e c².
- **Selection Rules**: ΔJ = 0, ±1 (no 0→0 for pure vector/axial). Parity change depends on transition type (Fermi vs Gamow-Teller).

### Gamma Decay
- **Process**: Excited nucleus emits photon. No change in A or Z.
- **Multipolarity**: Electric (E) or Magnetic (M) transitions. Dipole (L=1), Quadrupole (L=2), etc.
- **Selection Rules**: ΔJ ≤ L ≤ J_i + J_f. Parity change: (-1)^L for E, (-1)^{L+1} for M.
- **Internal Conversion**: Energy transferred to orbital electron instead of photon emission.

## Particle Physics Standard Model
### Quarks & Leptons
- **Quarks**: Up, Down, Charm, Strange, Top, Bottom. Color charge (RGB). Fractional charge (+2/3, -1/3).
- **Leptons**: Electron, Muon, Tau + corresponding neutrinos. No color charge. Integer charge (-1, 0).
- **Generations**: Three families. Mass increases with generation.

### Gauge Bosons
- **Photon (γ)**: Electromagnetic force. Massless, spin-1. Couples to charge.
- **W±, Z⁰**: Weak force. Massive (~80-90 GeV/c²). Short range. W couples to left-handed particles.
- **Gluons (g)**: Strong force. Massless, spin-1. Color charge (8 types). Confinement prevents free gluons.
- **Higgs Boson (H⁰)**: Scalar (spin-0). Gives mass to W, Z, quarks, leptons via Yukawa coupling. Mass ~125 GeV/c².

### Conservation Laws
- **Exact**: Energy, Momentum, Angular Momentum, Electric Charge, Color Charge, Baryon Number (approximate), Lepton Number (approximate).
- **Violated**: Parity (P) in weak interactions. CP violation in K and B meson decays.

### Feynman Diagrams
- **Vertices**: Interaction points. Conservation of charge, momentum at each vertex.
- **Lines**: Fermions (straight), Bosons (wavy/coiled).
- **Amplitude**: Calculate using Feynman rules. Square modulus gives probability/cross-section.
