# Optics & Wave Physics Reference

## Wave Properties
- **Wave Equation**: ∂²y/∂t² = v² ∂²y/∂x².
- **Speed**: v = fλ. Frequency f, Wavelength λ.
- **Superposition**: Resultant displacement is sum of individual displacements.
- **Interference**: Constructive (phase diff 0, 2π...) vs Destructive (phase diff π, 3π...).

## Light as Wave
- **Electromagnetic Spectrum**: Radio, Microwave, IR, Visible, UV, X-ray, Gamma.
- **Speed of Light**: c = 3×10⁸ m/s in vacuum. v = c/n in medium (n = refractive index).
- **Polarization**: Orientation of electric field oscillation. Linear, Circular, Elliptical.
- **Malus’ Law**: I = I₀ cos²θ. Intensity after polarizer.

## Interference Phenomena
- **Young’s Double Slit**:
  - Bright fringes: d sinθ = mλ.
  - Dark fringes: d sinθ = (m+½)λ.
  - Fringe spacing: Δy = λL/d.
- **Thin Film Interference**:
  - Phase shift upon reflection from higher-n medium.
  - Constructive: 2nt = (m+½)λ (if one phase shift).
  - Destructive: 2nt = mλ (if one phase shift).
- **Michelson Interferometer**: Measures small distances/wavelengths. Split beam, recombine.

## Diffraction
- **Huygens’ Principle**: Every point on wavefront acts as source of secondary wavelets.
- **Single Slit**:
  - Minima: a sinθ = mλ.
  - Central maximum width: 2λL/a.
- **Circular Aperture (Airy Disk)**:
  - First minimum: sinθ = 1.22 λ/D.
  - Rayleigh Criterion: Resolution limit θ_min = 1.22 λ/D.
- **Diffraction Grating**:
  - Maxima: d sinθ = mλ.
  - Higher resolution than double slit.

## Geometric Optics Approximation
- **Reflection**: θ_i = θ_r.
- **Refraction (Snell’s Law)**: n₁ sinθ₁ = n₂ sinθ₂.
- **Total Internal Reflection**: Occurs when n₁ > n₂ and θ₁ > θ_c (sinθ_c = n₂/n₁). Fiber optics.
- **Lenses**:
  - Thin Lens Equation: 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i.
  - Magnification: m = -d_i/d_o.
  - Converging (convex) vs Diverging (concave).
- **Mirrors**:
  - Spherical Mirror: 1/f = 1/d_o + 1/d_i. f = R/2.
  - Concave (converging) vs Convex (diverging).

## Dispersion & Absorption
- **Dispersion**: Refractive index depends on wavelength (n(λ)). Prism splits white light.
- **Cauchy’s Equation**: n(λ) = A + B/λ² + ...
- **Absorption**: Beer-Lambert Law: I = I₀ e^(-αx). α = absorption coefficient.
