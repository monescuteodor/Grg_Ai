# Particle Physics & Standard Model Reference

## Fundamental Particles
- **Quarks**: Up, Down, Charm, Strange, Top, Bottom.
  - Charge: +2/3 (u, c, t), -1/3 (d, s, b).
  - Color charge: Red, Green, Blue. Confined in hadrons.
- **Leptons**: Electron, Muon, Tau, Electron Neutrino, Muon Neutrino, Tau Neutrino.
  - Charge: -1 (e, μ, τ), 0 (ν).
  - No color charge.
- **Antiparticles**: Same mass, opposite charge. Positron (e⁺), Antiproton (p̄).

## Gauge Bosons (Force Carriers)
- **Photon (γ)**: Electromagnetic force. Massless. Infinite range.
- **W⁺, W⁻, Z⁰**: Weak force. Massive (~80-90 GeV). Short range (~10⁻¹⁸ m). Responsible for beta decay.
- **Gluons (g)**: Strong force. Massless. Color charge. Confined within hadrons. Short range due to confinement.
- **Higgs Boson (H⁰)**: Scalar particle. Gives mass to W, Z, quarks, leptons via Higgs field. Mass ~125 GeV.

## Hadrons
- **Baryons**: 3 quarks. Fermions.
  - Proton (uud): Stable. Charge +1.
  - Neutron (udd): Unstable outside nucleus. Charge 0. Beta decay: n → p + e⁻ + ν̄_e.
- **Mesons**: Quark + Antiquark. Bosons.
  - Pion (π⁺, π⁻, π⁰): Lightest mesons. Mediate residual strong force in nucleus.
  - Kaon (K): Contains strange quark. CP violation observed.

## Interactions & Conservation Laws
- **Strong Interaction**: Binds quarks in hadrons, nucleons in nucleus. Conserves color, flavor.
- **Electromagnetic Interaction**: Acts on charged particles. Conserves charge, parity.
- **Weak Interaction**: Changes flavor (quark/lepton type). Violates parity (P) and CP symmetry.
- **Gravity**: Negligible at particle scale. Not part of Standard Model.

## Experimental Physics
- **Accelerators**: LHC (Large Hadron Collider). Collide protons at high energy. Create new particles.
- **Detectors**: Track particles, measure energy/momentum.
  - Tracker: Measures path/curvature (momentum).
  - Calorimeter: Measures energy deposition.
  - Muon Chamber: Detects muons (penetrate other layers).
- **Cross-Section**: Probability of interaction. Measured in barns (1 b = 10⁻²⁸ m²).

## Beyond Standard Model
- **Dark Matter**: Does not interact electromagnetically. WIMPs, Axions candidates.
- **Dark Energy**: Accelerates universe expansion.
- **Supersymmetry (SUSY)**: Predicts partner particles for each SM particle. Not yet observed.
- **Grand Unified Theories (GUT)**: Unify Strong, Weak, EM forces at high energy.
