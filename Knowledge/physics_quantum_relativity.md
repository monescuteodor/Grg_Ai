# Quantum Mechanics & Relativity Reference

## Quantum Mechanics Formalism
### Wave Functions & Operators
- **Wave Function Ψ(x,t)**: Probability amplitude. |Ψ|² is probability density.
- **Normalization**: ∫|Ψ|²dx = 1. Total probability must be 1.
- **Expectation Value**: <A> = ∫Ψ* Â Ψ dx. Average result of measuring observable A.
- **Operators**: Position x̂ = x; Momentum p̂ = -iℏ∂/∂x; Energy Ĥ = -ℏ²/2m ∇² + V.

### Schrödinger Equation Applications
- **Infinite Square Well**: Eₙ = n²π²ℏ²/(2mL²). Ψₙ = √(2/L)sin(nπx/L). Quantized energy levels.
- **Harmonic Oscillator**: Eₙ = ℏω(n + 1/2). Equidistant energy levels. Zero-point energy E₀ = ℏω/2.
- **Hydrogen Atom**: Eₙ = -13.6 eV / n². Radial and angular parts. Quantum numbers n, l, m_l.
- **Tunneling**: Probability T ≈ e^(-2κL). κ = √(2m(V-E))/ℏ. Particle passes through barrier V > E.

### Spin & Angular Momentum
- **Spin Operators**: Ŝ², Ŝ_z. Eigenvalues s(s+1)ℏ², m_sℏ. Electron spin s=1/2.
- **Pauli Matrices**: σ_x, σ_y, σ_z. Represent spin-1/2 operators.
- **Addition of Angular Momentum**: J = L + S. Fine structure splitting.
- **Zeeman Effect**: Splitting of spectral lines in magnetic field due to spin/orbital interaction.

## Special Relativity
### Spacetime & Lorentz Transformations
- **Invariant Interval**: ds² = c²dt² - dx² - dy² - dz². Same in all inertial frames.
- **Lorentz Factor**: γ = 1/√(1-v²/c²).
- **Time Dilation**: Δt = γΔt₀. Moving clocks run slow.
- **Length Contraction**: L = L₀/γ. Moving objects shorten in direction of motion.
- **Velocity Addition**: u = (v+u')/(1+vu'/c²). Never exceeds c.

### Relativistic Dynamics
- **Relativistic Momentum**: p = γmv.
- **Total Energy**: E = γmc². Rest energy E₀ = mc². Kinetic energy K = (γ-1)mc².
- **Energy-Momentum Relation**: E² = (pc)² + (mc²)². For photons (m=0): E=pc.
- **Mass-Energy Equivalence**: Nuclear reactions convert mass defect Δm to energy ΔE=Δmc².

## General Relativity Basics
### Curved Spacetime
- **Equivalence Principle**: Gravity is indistinguishable from acceleration locally.
- **Geodesics**: Shortest path in curved spacetime. Free-falling particles follow geodesics.
- **Schwarzschild Metric**: Describes spacetime around spherical non-rotating mass.
- **Event Horizon**: Radius r_s = 2GM/c². Point of no return for black holes.
- **Gravitational Time Dilation**: Clocks run slower in stronger gravitational fields.
- **Gravitational Lensing**: Light bends around massive objects.
- **Gravitational Waves**: Ripples in spacetime caused by accelerating masses (e.g., merging black holes). Detected by LIGO.
