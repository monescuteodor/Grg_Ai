# CHAPTER 21: TROPICAL GEOMETRY & A1-HOMOTOPY


## Tropical Geometry

### Tropical Algebra

**Tropical Semiring:**
(ℝ ∪ {-∞}, ⊕, ⊙) where:
- a ⊕ b = max(a,b) (tropical addition)
- a ⊙ b = a + b (tropical multiplication)
- Identity: -∞ for ⊕, 0 for ⊙

**Valuation:**
For field K with non-archimedean valuation v: K* → ℝ:
v(a+b) ≥ min(v(a), v(b))
v(ab) = v(a) + v(b)

**Amoebas:**
For variety V ⊆ (ℂ*)ⁿ:
Amoeba(V) = {(log|z₁|, ..., log|zₙ|) : z ∈ V}
- Complement has convex components
- Spine = tropical variety

### Tropical Varieties

**Definition:**
Weighted polyhedral complex satisfying balancing condition.
- Pure dimension k
- Multiplicity on facets
- Balancing: Σ mᵢ vᵢ = 0 at each codim-1 face

**Fundamental Theorem:**
Tropical variety = non-archimedean amoeba = corner locus of tropical polynomials.

**Tropical Hypersurfaces:**
For tropical polynomial f = ⊕ cᵢ ⊙ x^{aᵢ}:
Trop(V(f)) = {x : maximum achieved at least twice}
- Dual to regular subdivision of Newton polytope

**Stable Intersection:**
For tropical varieties X, Y:
X ·_{st} Y = lim_{ε→0} (X + εv) ∩ Y
- Commutative, associative
- Bezout's theorem holds

## A¹-Homotopy Theory

### Motivic Spaces

**Nisnevich Topology:**
- Finer than Zariski, coarser than étale
- Excision property
- Mayer-Vietoris for distinguished squares

**Motivic Spaces:**
- Simplicial presheaves on smooth schemes
- A¹-localization: identify A¹ with point
- Bousfield localization

**Motivic Homotopy Category:**
H(k) = unstable motivic homotopy category
- Objects: motivic spaces
- A¹-weak equivalences

### Stable Motivic Homotopy Theory

**Motivic Sphere:**
S^{p,q} = (S¹)^{∧p} ∧ (𝔾ₘ)^{∧q}
- p = topological degree
- q = Tate twist

**Stable Category SH(k):**
- Motivic spectra
- T-suspension: T = ℙ¹ ≃ S^{2,1}
- Six operations

**Motivic Cohomology:**
H^{p,q}(X, ℤ) = [X, K(ℤ(q), p)]
- K(ℤ(q), p): Eilenberg-MacLane motivic spectrum
- Beilinson-Lichtenbaum conjecture

### Motivic Steenrod Algebra

**Operations:**
- Reduced power operations
- Bockstein
- Cartan formula
- Adem relations

**Dual Steenrod Algebra:**
- Motivic analogue of classical dual
- Structure theorems

### Slice Filtration

**Definition:**
For E ∈ SH(k):
f_q E = local piece with homotopy concentrated in degrees ≥ q
- s_q E = q-th slice
- Slice spectral sequence

**Applications:**
- Algebraic K-theory
- Hermitian K-theory
- Algebraic cobordism

### Milnor Conjecture & Motivic Theory

**Milnor Conjecture (proved by Voevodsky):**
K_n^M(F)/2 ≅ H^n_{ét}(F, ℤ/2ℤ)
- Motivic cohomology with ℤ/2 coefficients
- Proof uses motivic Steenrod operations

**Bloch-Kato Conjecture:**
K_n^M(F)/l ≅ H^n_{ét}(F, μ_l^{⊗n})
- Proved by Voevodsky, Rost, Weibel
- Norm varieties
- Rost motives
