# Finance & accounting

Reference for accountants/bookkeepers and finance work. General concepts, not tax advice — rates, thresholds and deadlines are jurisdiction-specific and change; always verify the current local figures and have a licensed accountant/tax advisor confirm anything filed. Never invent specific rates or deadlines.

## Bookkeeping basics
- **Double-entry**: every transaction hits two accounts; debits = credits. Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
- **Chart of accounts**: assets, liabilities, equity, income, expenses — keep categories consistent.
- **Accrual vs cash basis**: accrual records when earned/incurred; cash when money moves. State which you use.

## The three financial statements (and how they link)
- **Profit & Loss (income statement)**: revenue − expenses = profit over a period.
- **Balance sheet**: snapshot at a date — assets, liabilities, equity.
- **Cash flow**: cash in/out (operating, investing, financing). Profit ≠ cash.
- Link: net profit flows into equity (retained earnings); cash flow reconciles profit to the cash balance on the balance sheet.

## VAT & invoicing (concepts)
- A compliant invoice usually needs: seller & buyer details + tax IDs, invoice number & date, description, quantity, net amount, VAT rate & amount, total. Verify the exact local requirements.
- VAT = output VAT (on sales) − input VAT (on purchases) = payable/refundable. Confirm the current local rate(s) and registration threshold — do not assume.

## Expenses & categorisation
- Separate business vs personal; keep receipts; categorise consistently (COGS vs operating vs capital).
- **Capex vs opex**: capital assets are depreciated over time; operating costs are expensed now.
- **Depreciation/amortisation**: spread an asset's cost over its useful life (straight-line = cost / years).

## Useful numbers / ratios
- Gross margin = (revenue − COGS) / revenue. Net margin = net profit / revenue.
- Burn rate & runway = cash / monthly net burn. Break-even = fixed costs / contribution margin.
- Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities (liquidity).

## Worked examples
- **P&L line check**: "Revenue 10,000; COGS 4,000; opex 3,500 → gross 6,000 (60%); net 2,500 (25%)." Show the steps.
- **VAT (generic rate r)**: net 1,000 at rate r → VAT = 1,000 × r; gross = 1,000 × (1+r). Ask for the local rate instead of guessing.
- **Depreciation**: asset 12,000, 5-year life, straight-line → 2,400/year.

## Data handling
Financial records contain personal/sensitive data — treat as confidential, keep placeholders verbatim in private mode, apply retention limits, and never expose client financials in logs or shared links.

## Output style
Show the calculation and assumptions, label the period/currency, keep categories consistent, and clearly flag anything that depends on current local tax law or needs a licensed professional before filing.
