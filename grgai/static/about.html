The problem

The people who need AI most on their hardest work are the ones who can't use it

Everyone uses ChatGPT and Claude today. But there's a whole group of people who simply can't — not because they don't want to, but because their most valuable material isn't allowed to leave their machine.

A developer has proprietary code, keys, a client's infrastructure. A lawyer, accountant, HR or healthcare professional has regulated personal data (GDPR). Paste a contract full of names, national IDs and IBANs into a US AI service and you've broken the rules and risked a fine. So they either avoid AI on the work that matters — or take a quiet risk.

The insight

You don't win on a "smarter AI". You win on control.

The big players can't easily fix this — their whole model is "send us everything, we process it in our cloud." And we're not trying to out-model them; we use the same Claude and GPT anyone can.

We don't sell intelligence. We sell control and confidentiality — the AI you can use on what you can't paste into ChatGPT.

That's a space the giants leave wide open, because closing it would work against how they're built.

Who it's for

Two people, one promise

Developers

who can't ship their code to a third party

GrgCode runs on your own machine. It reads, writes and runs your code — but file writes and commands need your approval, with a diff before every change. Your codebase stays local; only what the model actually needs is sent. Control, not "I handed everything to the cloud."

Sensitive data · GDPR

lawyers, finance, HR, healthcare

Turn on a private agent and Grg AI redacts the sensitive data in what you type — names, emails, national IDs, IBANs, cards, secrets — on your device, before anything is sent. The model works on placeholders; your real values are restored only on your screen.

The common thread: both have something they can't leak — and Grg AI is the one that gives them AI without forcing them to hand it over.

How we keep the promise

Every feature serves one thing: your data stays yours

  • On-device redaction — names, emails, national IDs, IBANs, cards and secrets are replaced with placeholders before they ever leave your device.
  • Specialist agents — pick an expert for your field (legal, GDPR, dev, security and more), or build your own.
  • GrgCode runs locally — approval on every file write and command, diffs, checkpoints and undo. Your code never lives on our servers.
  • EU hosting & isolation — TLS everywhere, strict access rules, and published pages sandboxed in an isolated origin.
  • Zero-persistence private sessions — what's sensitive isn't saved.
