Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Effective immediately

Summary: We collect your email and conversation history to provide the service. We never sell your data. You can delete everything at any time.

1. Who We Are

Grg AI is a self-hosted AI programming assistant created and operated by Monescu Teodor, based in Brașov, Romania. Contact: teodor.monescu@icloud.com

2. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used for authentication only
  • Display name — shown in the interface
  • Conversation history — your chats with Grg AI, stored in Firebase Firestore
  • Account metadata — account creation date, last sign-in time (managed by Firebase Authentication)

When you use Grg AI without an account (guest mode), we store only a local message counter in your browser. No data is sent to our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate you and keep you logged in across devices
  • To sync your conversation history across your devices
  • To improve the service (aggregate, anonymized usage patterns only)

We do not use your data to train AI models, serve ads, or sell to third parties.

4. Data Storage

Firebase Authentication — your email and password hash are stored by Google Firebase, governed by Google's privacy policy.

Firebase Firestore — your conversation history is stored in Google's cloud infrastructure in the EU region. Only you can read or write your own data (enforced by Firestore security rules).

AI Processing — your messages are sent to our self-hosted AI model running on our own hardware. Messages are processed in memory and not permanently logged server-side.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication and database. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare — network tunneling and DDoS protection. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • DuckDuckGo — web search (when triggered). Queries are sent without personal identifiers. DuckDuckGo Privacy Policy

6. Data Retention

Your data is stored for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all associated data (conversations, profile) is permanently deleted within 30 days.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user in Romania/EU, you have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your account and all data
  • Portability — export your conversations via the app
  • Objection — object to processing; you can stop using the service at any time
  • Restriction — request we limit processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at teodor.monescu@icloud.com with subject "GDPR Request".

8. Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • Passwords are hashed by Firebase — we never see your plain-text password
  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Firestore security rules ensure users can only access their own data
  • Our server is protected by Cloudflare

9. Children's Privacy

Grg AI is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. We will notify users via the app for significant changes. The "Last updated" date above will always reflect the most recent version.

11. Contact

For any privacy questions: teodor.monescu@icloud.com
