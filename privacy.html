Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Overview

Grg AI is a self-hosted AI programming assistant created by Monescu Teodor. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Conversations: All conversations are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. They are never sent to or stored on our servers. The server only processes your messages in real-time to generate responses — nothing is saved server-side.

Server Logs: Basic HTTP access logs (IP address, timestamp, request path) may be temporarily stored for debugging purposes. These are automatically deleted after 7 days.

No Tracking: We do not use cookies for tracking, analytics services, or any third-party data collection tools.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Account credentials or passwords
  • Payment information
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • File contents uploaded through the attachment feature (processed in-memory only)

How We Use Data

Your messages are sent to our self-hosted AI model to generate responses. The model runs locally on our own hardware — no data is sent to OpenAI, Google, or any third-party AI provider.

Data Storage

  • Client-side: Conversation history is stored in your browser's localStorage. You can clear it at any time through Settings → Clear All Data.
  • Server-side: No conversation data is stored. Messages are processed in memory and discarded after the response is generated.

Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare: We use Cloudflare for DNS and tunneling. Cloudflare may process connection metadata according to their own privacy policy.
  • DuckDuckGo: When web search is triggered, queries are sent to DuckDuckGo. See DuckDuckGo's privacy policy for details.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access your data — all your data is in your browser, fully under your control
  • Delete your data — clear localStorage or use Settings → Clear All Data
  • Data portability — use Settings → Export to download your conversations as JSON
  • Object to processing — you can stop using the service at any time

Children's Privacy

Grg AI does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The service is designed as a programming assistant and is suitable for all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
